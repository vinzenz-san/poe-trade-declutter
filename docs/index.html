PoE Trade Declutter

PoE Trade Declutter is a free browser extension for Firefox and Chrome that lets you hide unwanted filter categories and individual filters on the Path of Exile trade site's search page — reversible with one click.

What it does

The official Path of Exile trade site shows every filter category and every filter within it, whether you use it or not. PoE Trade Declutter lets you hide the ones you don't, so the search panel only shows what you actually use.

Hide whole filter categories

Sanctum Filters, Heist Filters, and anything else you never touch — collapse them out of the way with one click, and bring them back just as easily.

Hide individual filters

Keep a category open but tuck away the specific fields you never search with, like "Base Percentile" under Miscellaneous, without losing the ones you do use.

Everything is reversible

A "Hidden filters (N)" toggle per category, and a global "Expand all hidden" button, let you review or restore anything you've hidden at any time.

Privacy focused

No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics. Preferences stay in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. Open source under MIT.
