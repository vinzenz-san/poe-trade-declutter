PoE Trade Declutter

A free browser extension for Firefox and Chrome that lets you hide unwanted filter categories and filters on the Path of Exile trade site's search page.

Hide categories and filters

Collapse whole filter categories (Sanctum Filters, Heist Filters, ...) or just the individual fields you never use, right from the trade site's own filter panel — bring anything back just as easily.
