Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16

PoE Trade Declutter is a browser extension (Firefox & Chrome) that lets you hide or reorder filter categories and stat filters on the official Path of Exile trade site (pathofexile.com/trade). This policy explains what the extension accesses and why.

Summary

  • The extension does not operate a server, does not use analytics, and does not transmit any data anywhere.
  • Your preferences (which categories and filters you've marked hidden) are stored only in your browser's local extension storage (browser.storage.local), via the standard storage permission. This data never leaves your device.
  • The extension only runs on pathofexile.com/trade and pathofexile.com/trade2 pages. It does not run on, read, or access any other website.
  • The extension reads data already present on the trade page — its own filter definitions and your existing session settings, both stored by the Path of Exile trade site itself in your browser's localStorage — purely to know which categories and filters currently exist, so it can add show/hide controls next to them. Nothing read this way is copied, stored elsewhere, or sent off your device.
  • No account, sign-in, or personal information is required or collected.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it's needed
storage Saves your chosen hidden categories/filters locally so they persist between visits to the trade site.
Host access to pathofexile.com/trade* Required to inject the show/hide controls into the trade site's filter panel. The extension does not run on any other page.

Third parties

PoE Trade Declutter is an independent, unofficial project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games. It does not share data with Grinding Gear Games, advertisers, or any other third party — it has no mechanism to do so.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the change will be reflected in the extension's GitHub repository.
